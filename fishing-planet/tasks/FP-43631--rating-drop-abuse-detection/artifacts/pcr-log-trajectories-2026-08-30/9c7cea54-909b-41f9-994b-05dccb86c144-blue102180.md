### Caveats on the mechanism

Two things this log establishes and one it does not.

Established: the *net effect* is a repeating loop - absence keeps registration-time PCR inside NOOBS, the sessions he actually fishes are near-always top-three, and the resulting spike above 100 is always erased by absence before the next entry batch. Established too: the registration bursts follow the settlement batches that dropped him, sometimes within a minute (`2026-08-16T21:55:18Z` -> `21:56:00Z`; `2026-08-24T13:52:52Z` -> `13:53:34Z`; `2026-08-26T17:06:04Z` -> `17:08:05Z`).

Not established: deliberate sequencing.
