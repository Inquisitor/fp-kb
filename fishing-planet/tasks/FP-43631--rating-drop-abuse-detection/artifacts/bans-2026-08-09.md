## The rule that changed

Rule 1 was rewritten this cycle from an absolute definition ("farming the NOOBS bracket") to a
bracket-relative one: taking prizes in a bracket below the one the player's own play would place
him in, having arrived there by shedding rating. Two limbs must both hold -- **chosen descent**
(rating earned in play, given back through absence, with the temporal order visible in the
ledger) and **payoff below the ceiling**. Rule 7 direction 2, which allowed upper-bracket
candidates to be closed as non-targets, is withdrawn; both closures made under it are reversed.

The change came out of re-examining the group the old rule excluded. Measured over the cumulative
window, that group turned out to earn strongly positive rating from playing (+440 to +1822) while
losing comparable amounts purely to absence (-581 to -1436), converting two to three times better
in the bracket below than in the one above, and clustering just under the 1000 boundary. Same
mechanism as NOOBS farming, one floor up.

**The rewrite did not become a blanket.** Of five upper-bracket candidates only Martino_CZ was
banned. EsseDouble, TrcikLowFiv and RoBot-lau failed the temporal-order requirement -- aggregate
signs present, sequence absent. Tyrant-Kraken and PgS_DaveSon shed rating but cash in the bracket
they play in, which the rule expressly reserves for WATCH. x.Anastasia.x plays and wins entirely
in TOPS.

## Costaz_Rexyus -- verdict overturned after the review

The review returned WATCH. The judge read the 900 -> 0 collapse as one 76-hour absence rather
than a cycle, and concluded the player had simply stopped playing and met a stack of standing
registrations. That reading is now known to be wrong.

Registration events carry timestamps and were in the dump all along, but no rule consumed them.
Clustering registrations into sessions and asking how many contained no play within six hours
either side gives **19 registration-only visits** for this account -- nineteen separate occasions
of logging in, registering and not playing. Measured separately, the forward registration horizon
is short: across 1080 paired events the median lead to competition end is 9.5 hours, the 95th
percentile 22, and nothing exceeds 43. A 76-hour gap therefore cannot be covered by a single act
of over-registration; it requires repeated deliberate returns.

Verdict corrected to BAN. No database action needed -- Support already holds him to 2026-09-09,
which is longer than our tariff. The Support dissent this created is therefore resolved in
Support's favour, retrospectively and from data already held, rather than by waiting on a player
who is banned and cannot reappear in the cohort.

## The signal, and its limits

The registration-visit count is strong evidence when present and says nothing when absent.
Dr.Seven shows only one such visit and is a confident ban: he registers in bulk inside a playing
session and chooses which events to attend. Ranked across the cohort: Costaz_Rexyus 19,
ShadowOfOxigen 11, Martino_CZ 10, martelli04 7, RoBot-lau 7, switch-toad 7, then a tail.
Tyrant-Kraken records zero across 58 registration sessions, which strengthens his WATCH.

It belongs in the trajectory card as a fact for the advocates to argue over, not as another
threshold. The lesson is about card design rather than agent reasoning: the judges did reason
well within what they were given -- rejecting a "surgical two-hour absence" reading as a session
ending, discounting a same-second flush as a server artifact rather than player awareness -- but
they could not weigh evidence that the parser discarded.

**Two defects found during verification and fixed in the methodology.**

The verify script aggregated across all periods, which would have returned OK even if only the
new week were banned while the closing week -- the one the imminent reward run pays out on --

once. Caught only because the operator flagged the rollover.

The leaderboard sync had to be run twice: one Steam and two PS accounts registered into
competitions that finished just after 00:00 UTC and were written into fresh, unbanned rows of the
new period. Whether the row-creation path reads the profile ban flag is unresolved; until it is,
the sweep for effectively-banned players holding open leaderboard rows should run before each
reward payout, not only at ban time.
