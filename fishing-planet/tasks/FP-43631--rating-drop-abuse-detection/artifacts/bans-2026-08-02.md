# Executed bans -- 2026-08-03 (FP-43631 follow-up week 13)

Seven players banned. The cohort came back at **18** -- a third consecutive slow decline
(20 -> 19 -> 18). Two things changed structurally this cycle: the ban durations were raised to
**4 weeks first offence / 8 weeks recidivism**, and **rule 6 was rewritten from a leniency into
an aggravator** after an A/B measurement showed it was the mechanism behind the review's
softness.

## The rule-6 measurement

The review was run **twice over the same 18 candidates with the same evidence**, once under the
previous rules and once under the revision. In both runs the pre-trial context was
**Support-blind** -- the judges were told nothing about who Support had already banned -- so the
comparison against Support's independent decisions is a real test rather than a confirmation.

| | Previous rules | Revised |
|---|---|---|
| BAN / WATCH / EXONERATE | 11 / 6 / 1 | **14 / 3 / 1** |
| Missed a player Support banned | **2** | **0** |

Three verdicts moved, all from WATCH to BAN: **ELPEZGORDO12**, **ZacKasoN** (both independently
banned by Support, both released under rule 6) and **Old-Black-Fisher**. Nothing moved the other
way -- no confident BAN was destabilised.

The two candidates that stayed on WATCH held for the right reasons: **eMadkiller5964** on rule 3
with 7 competitions played against 19 no-shows, and **Pilou625057** on flavor mismatch with 9 of
18 played competitions in TOPS. Both legitimate defenses survived, which is the check that the
rewrite did not simply turn the review into a rubber stamp.

**Why the rule was withdrawn.** Across weeks 8-13 its deference branch produced twelve WATCH
verdicts and not one was ever vindicated. Every traceable case ended in a ban -- CreekSamurai,
evgeniy3311, MORPH3US, VGB_N4rkos060905, Zemaro, vlad_spain, TurboBandz6351, BOOMDATRUTH2,
La_Iena_River_. The thirteenth, FarantirPL, had been recorded as vindicated *by his absence from
the next cohort*; he returned this cycle at a 70% no-show share with PCR 0, which is what
retired absence as evidence of anything.

The replacement inverts the reading. The wide gate already demands six no-shows, a 30% share,
-90 rating and more than three prizes, so nobody reaching review is a bystander. A low lifetime
prize count next to a high in-window haul now means the player's entire competitive record
*consists of* the conduct under review -- MORPH3US went from 6 lifetime prizes to 18 in the
interval; LuizFernandoo took 9 of his 45 inside a 20-day window at a 69% conversion rate he
never approached in the bracket he actually held.

## Duration change

Raised to 4W / 8W. The 2W term was not deterring: strullendorfer returned 13 days after his ban
lapsed, jackylu 16 days, kokoljj about three weeks, Aorney about four and a half. The CS lead
proposed permanent bans for repeat offenders; 4W/8W is the agreed interim step.

Worth noting from the ban log: **Support's own practice is split** -- one operator issues one
month, another two weeks, in the same week on the same kind of case. That is an operator
difference rather than a policy, and is worth aligning separately.
