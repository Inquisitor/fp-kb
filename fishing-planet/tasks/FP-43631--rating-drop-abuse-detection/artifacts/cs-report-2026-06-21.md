    Support coverage already exceeds our 2W standard.

  1. MASTERS/TOPS-only sandbagging flavor — not our NOOBS-farming methodology target
  2. Mixed N+M flavor with net-positive PCR — deferred for another cycle of observation

`IsBanned=true` on a row means actively banned at scan time. A row with `IsBanned=true` and
`BanEnd` in the past or NULL is a stale flag — the player is effectively NOT banned per the
game-engine `IsCompetitionsBannedNow()` check. One stale-flag row this week: Tight_LinesJoe65
(WATCH, BanEnd 2025-05-10 — over a year ago).

> Per-bracket cells use `N / M / T` **with spaces** on purpose — `8/12/0` gets auto-parsed as a date by Google Sheets.
