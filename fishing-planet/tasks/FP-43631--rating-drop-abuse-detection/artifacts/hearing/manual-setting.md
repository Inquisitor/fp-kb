# Manual for the hearing

This is the working manual of the operator who reviews these cases every week: the setting below,
then his reading guide as it stands. The guide says what he looks for when he decides a case from a
trajectory card. It is a description of behaviour to think with, not a checklist.

## The setting

Fishing Planet runs a competition every 2 hours. A player registers in advance, then either comes
and plays or does not. Each player has a competition rating (PCR). The rating at the start of a
competition sets his bracket, and he meets only opponents of that bracket: NOOBS 0-100, MIDDLES
101-1000, TOPS 1001+. All times are UTC, written 22:00Z.

- A prize place (1st to 3rd) adds roughly 20 to 55 points. Places just below add a little, places
  11 to 13 change nothing, low places cost a few points.
- Not coming to a competition he registered for (NO-SHOW) costs 10 to 20 points, depending on the
  competition; the expensive ones cost 20.
- Entering and scoring nothing (ZERO-SCORE) costs about half of an absence.
- Unregistering before the start costs nothing.
- The weekly leaderboard counts 1st places. Its top 10 receive a reward when the week closes.

Verdicts: **BAN** (competition ban), **WATCH** (not banned, read first next week), **CLEAR** (no
action). The length of a ban is set by the ban history, not by how brazen the case is, and is not
part of the verdict.

The examples in the guide carry no names; they are real cases of earlier weeks.
