## Two defects found mid-cycle

**Rule 1(b) regression.** The week-14 rewrite made the offence bracket-relative and thereby
exempted the most deflated accounts: with no bracket above them inside the window there was
nothing for their prizes to be "below", and the same-bracket carve-out sheltered them. Six of the
ten first-hearing WATCH verdicts were this shape. Rule 1(b) now takes the ceiling as the higher of
actual exposure and the counterfactual — the bracket the player's rating would occupy without his
absence penalties. Re-heard: 6 of 7 flipped to BAN, Leelos90's WATCH upheld. Counterfactual
ceilings against actual rating: loseloop 1090 vs 0, TrcikLowFiv 1122 vs 762, Saintmnk 528 vs 10,
SoRA6r 445 vs 62, ScionHades8639 486 vs 0, HIflyfishingGH 414 vs 34.

**Ledger retention is fourteen days.** Every card began 2026-08-16 against a window opening
2026-08-10, and the whole Steam collection spans exactly that fortnight. Findings phrased as "no
boundary crossing" were true of the ledger and false of the record — TrcikLowFiv's first six days
held eight TOPS entries of which six were no-shows, none of it visible. SQL is complete and now
governs anything older than two weeks.

## BracketId is not a rating band

Raised by a judge who refused to convict on the label, and confirmed against the GDD.
`TournamentParticipants.BracketId` is the bucket after matchmaking balancing: undersized buckets
pull from neighbours and then merge, preferring the stronger neighbour and falling back to the
weaker. A rating of 20562 was found sitting in bucket 1. Agreement with the standard bands is
about 92%, with misses clustered in thin or oddly-configured competitions.

All 11 first-hearing bans were re-derived from `CompetitionRatingAtStart`. Ten survived unchanged
or strengthened; **tigrou_le_boss42 was withdrawn** — his 1(b) rested explicitly "on the SQL's own
tier field", and by rating only 3 of his 6 prizes were taken at or below 100 while 26 of his 31
starts were above it. Rule 2 does not ban on a weak pattern.

Every disagreement ran the same direction: the label overstated the share won in the bottom
bracket, which is what balancing predicts. `Prizes_NMT` and `Played_NMT` in the CS tables are now
computed from rating; the column names are unchanged and `MaxRatingAtStart` was appended at the
end so existing sheets keep working.
