# FP-43631 week-18 -- Community & Support handoff, sweep 2026-08-31..2026-09-06

17 accounts were reviewed. 9 were found to be shedding competition rating in order to take prizes
below their level; 8 of them were banned here and the 9th already carried a ban applied elsewhere.
The 3 TSVs are the data for the shared sheet, one tab per platform as usual.

Rating bands, since the tables use them throughout: **NOOBS** is rating 0-100, **MIDDLES** is
101-1000, **TOPS** is 1001 and above.

## What changed in detection this cycle

Up to now the sweep looked only for **no-shows** -- registering and never starting. From this cycle
it looks for **unproductive participation**, which is no-shows *plus* finishes where the player
started and scored nothing at all. Both shed competition rating; entering and catching nothing costs
roughly half what not appearing costs, so a player needs about twice as many of them to sink the
same distance.

This is not a theoretical addition. **Myky0576** is on the list precisely because of it: 42
registrations, 41 starts, **1** no-show and 27 finishes with nothing scored. His absence rate is
2.38% -- far below anything the old sweep would look at twice -- while two thirds of his entries
produced no result at all. The previous sweep would not have picked him up.

Measured across August on all 3 platforms, the change raises how many capable bottom-bracket
harvesters the sweep catches from 70 to 91 out of 109, with nothing lost, at the cost of roughly a
third more accounts to review each week.

A note on disqualifications, because the tables can mislead here. A start that ends in a
disqualification is not counted as a zero-score finish. A **no-show is counted regardless** of
whether the account was disqualified, so a disqualification can sit inside the `NoShows` figure and
therefore inside `UnproductiveSharePct`. Leelos90 is the live case: his single disqualification is
one of the 26 no-shows behind his 74%.

## Reading the tables

Column order:

`UserId, Username, Platform, Level, Rank, CurrentPCR, Registrations, Started, ZeroScore, NoShows,
NoShowSharePct, Unproductive, UnproductiveSharePct, RatingFromUnproductive, RatingFromNoShow_DQ,
RatingFromProductivePlay, RatingNetDelta, Gold, Silver, Bronze, Disqualifications, LifetimeGold,
LifetimeSilver, LifetimeBronze, Played_NMT, Prizes_NMT, TotalPrizes, IsBanned, BanEnd, Verdict,
MaxRatingAtStart`

New this cycle are `Unproductive`, `UnproductiveSharePct` and `RatingFromUnproductive`. They sit
between `NoShowSharePct` and `RatingFromNoShow_DQ`, so **every column after them has moved 3
places to the right** compared with last week's tab. `RatingFromRealPlay` has also been renamed
`RatingFromProductivePlay` and no longer includes zero-score finishes, so it is not the same figure
as the column of the old name. `MaxRatingAtStart` is unchanged and was already the last column last
cycle.

- **`Verdict`** has 2 values only. **BANNED** means banned from competitions, **WATCH** means
  reviewed and not banned this time.
- **`Played_NMT` / `Prizes_NMT`** read as NOOBS / MIDDLES / TOPS -- where the player competes, and
  where they actually take prizes. The gap between the two is the whole point of this task. These
  are computed from the rating each player carried into each competition, not from the group label
  the matchmaker assigned, because the matchmaker moves people between groups to fill them and the
  label is therefore not a reliable statement of anyone's level.
- **`Unproductive` / `UnproductiveSharePct`** are the new headline figures: registrations that
  produced no competitive result, and their share of everything the player signed up for.
- **`RatingFromProductivePlay`** is the net rating from competitions the player started and scored
  in -- net, so a poor result inside it counts against him. Read it against `RatingFromUnproductive`:
  a large positive next to a large negative is what makes an account worth looking at. It is not by
  itself the finding; that rests on the order the events happened in.
- **`MaxRatingAtStart`** is the highest rating the player carried *into* a competition during the
  week. It is not his weekly peak -- a win can lift him higher immediately afterwards, and often
  does.
- **`Rank`** is a profile field, not a leaderboard placing. Where this report mentions a position on
  the weekly board, that comes from the leaderboard and will not match this column.
- **`RatingFromNoShow_DQ`** covers no-shows and disqualifications only, which is why it differs from
  `RatingFromUnproductive`.
- The `N / M / T` cells carry spaces around the slashes because Sheets otherwise reads `8/12/0` as a
  date.
