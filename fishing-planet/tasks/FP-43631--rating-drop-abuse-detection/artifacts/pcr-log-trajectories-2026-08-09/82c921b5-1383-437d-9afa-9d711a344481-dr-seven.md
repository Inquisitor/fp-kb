# PCR trajectory — Dr.Seven (Steam)

## Verdict

**Textbook harvest sawtooth.** The played/absent/prize order is present and repeats five times
inside eight days. Play lifts PCR to just over the NOOBS ceiling, a no-show batch drops it straight
back under 100, and essentially every prize is then taken from a NOOBS-bracket entry: **10 of the
11 top-3 finishes in the dump were entered at PCR <= 100**; the single exception (#329455, entered
at PCR 107) is on day one before the cycle stabilises. PCR never gets near the MIDDLES ceiling —
lifetime max in the window is 144 — so the 1000 boundary is never in play.

## Anomalies and logging artifacts

- **Floor clamp, visible.** 2026-08-05 02:00:07, comp #329500: printed delta **-15** but the pair
  reads **14 -> 0**. Real loss 14. Reading the printed delta here would understate PCR by 1.
- **Penalty swallowed at the floor.** Comp #329501 was registered 2026-08-05 01:33:13, processed
  2026-08-06 03:56:54 with a blank `Place:` (a no-show) and produced **no ledger line at all** —
  PCR was already 0. This is the documented floor artifact, not inactivity: the sparse stretch
  between 08-05 02:00 and 08-06 06:00 contains at least one uncounted no-show.
- **Played, no ledger line.** Comp #329515: scoring started 2026-08-06 08:02:13, processed
  10:00:12 with a blank `Place:`. PCR was 72 both before (after #329514) and after (before
  #329516), so the result was a genuine zero delta rather than a clamp. 26 scoring starts, 25
  ledger lines.
- **Registered, never processed in window.** Comp #329540, registered 2026-08-07 23:53:12; its
  result falls after the dump ends.
- **Registration bursts.** Registrations arrive in tight bundles seconds apart (e.g. 08-01
  08:35:03 / :07 / :11 / :15 / :19 for #329458-#329462 — all five later no-showed and all five
  land in the same 08-02 04:27:59 flush). No `FAILED: Register in competition` lines.
- **Anti-cheat noise is extreme.** 326 `CHEAT:` triggers across the window — 100 "Line has high
  extension too often", 59 "Throw. Same player position and rotation N times in row" (bot-like
  input), 56 "Friction force is too high", 39+20 "Fish catch distance is long / VERY long",
  32 "Undriven boat moves TOO fast", 10 "Tackle is further than max cast length". The
  same-position-and-rotation throws and the boat-speed violations are the ones that read as
  automation rather than latency.
- **9 distinct IPs and 7 distinct MACs** across 26 scoring sessions (107.151.183.69,
  107.151.183.84, 107.151.235.92, 107.151.235.108, 107.151.235.111, 38.77.225.170, 38.77.225.221,
  43.159.140.118, 66.160.191.3) — consistent with a farm rather than a single household client.
