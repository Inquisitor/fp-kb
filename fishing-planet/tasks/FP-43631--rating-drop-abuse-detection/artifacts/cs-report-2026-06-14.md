## Verdict legend

Verdict in the TSV is reduced to two values to give a clean picture for Support:

- **`BANNED`** — comp-banned by us this cycle. Profile + LB + Mongo banLog flipped; `BanEnd` is
  2026-06-29 (NEW, 2W) or 2026-07-13 (REPEAT, 4W). Support decides durable-ban duration on top.
- **`WATCH`** — we reviewed and chose not to ban. Three reasons (see Reading notes per row):
  1. Adversarial-trial verdict — pattern present but insufficient under our methodology (no MIDDLES
     exposure, net-positive trajectory, or MIDDLES-veteran lifetime profile)
  2. TOP-only or MIDDLES-only flavor — different mechanism, not our NOOBS-farming enforcement target
  3. Heavy-veteran profile (lifetime 50+ prizes) — real top-tier player having a high-activity week

`IsBanned=true` on a WATCH row does NOT necessarily mean the player is currently banned. The game-engine canonical check (`ProfileLogic.IsCompetitionsBannedNow`) is the AND of `IsCompetitionsBanned=true` **and** `CompetitionsBanEndDate > now`. So a row with `IsBanned=true` plus a `BanEnd` in the past OR an empty `BanEnd` is a **stale flag** — the player is effectively NOT banned. Two such rows in this report (VM_NPWP and IFC_BaysEmperor) are explained in the Reading notes below.

> Per-bracket cells use `N / M / T` **with spaces** on purpose — `8/12/0` gets auto-parsed as a date by Google Sheets.
