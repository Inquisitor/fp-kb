# Community & Support report -- 2026-08-09 (FP-43631 week 14)

Sunday sweep for the window **2026-08-03 -> 2026-08-09**. Cohort came back at 19 after three
declining weeks (20 -> 19 -> 18 -> 19), so the slow drain has flattened. We banned **9**; Support
had already actioned **2** before the sweep, both confirmed by the review.

**What changed in how we judge these cases.** Until this week the rule targeted farming in the
lowest bracket by name, and players whose prizes sat in the middle or top brackets were set aside
as "a different mechanism". That was wrong. The same behaviour works at every bracket boundary:
win where you are, shed the rating your wins earn by not showing up, stay below the line, keep
winning. The rule is now stated relative to the bracket a player's own results would place him
in, not relative to the lowest bracket. One ban this cycle (Martino_CZ) comes from that change.

The rule did not become a blanket. Of the five upper-bracket candidates only one was banned; the
others either shed rating but collect their prizes in the same bracket they play in, or show no
deliberate descent at all.
