## Reading notes

- Verdict is **BANNED** (flag set, end date ahead) or **WATCH**.
- 2 rows carry a ban flag whose end date has passed and who are therefore **not** restricted
  in-game: tigrou_le_boss42 (2026-07-13) and CFC-T-W-T-32568 (2026-08-03).
- **`Played_NMT` and `Prizes_NMT` changed meaning this week -- same columns, better source.** They
  used to be split by the bracket label the database records against each entry. That label is the
  group a player ends up in after matchmaking balances the field, which is usually but not always
  his rating band -- undersized groups pull players in from neighbouring ones. Both columns are now
  computed from the player's actual rating when the competition started, which is exact. For most
  people the 2 readings agree; where they differ the old label overstated how much was won at the
  bottom.
- **`MaxRatingAtStart` is new and is appended at the end**, so existing sheets keep working. It is
  the highest rating he carried into any competition that week -- useful for seeing how far above
  his collection point he actually gets.
- Cells `N / M / T` keep spaces around the slash so Sheets does not date-parse them.
- TrcikLowFiv shows almost no activity in the charged week because his case rests on the 3 weeks as
  a whole; he was on watch from the previous cycle.
