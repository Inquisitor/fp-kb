Surgical ban of seventeen players hand-confirmed as rating-drop abusers via Mongo Tournament-log
trajectory analysis, then put through a 78-agent adversarial trial (prosecutor / defense / judge

Of the 21 BAN verdicts, **4 were already actioned by Support before our sweep** (Kacumi, poink,

**Two key calibration observations this week:**

1. **Week-6 trial WATCH on Kacumi was wrong**. Our week-6 adversarial trial returned WATCH conf 7
   on the basis of net +94 PCR over 3 weeks and "climbing into MIDDLES" trajectory. Within a week
   Support banned her durably, and the week-7 trajectory (10 batched flushes + 4 MIDDLES drops +
   24.5h streak + PCR drained 38->14) confirmed the bracket-farming signature was always real.
   The week-6 trial defense argument was that climbs are anti-farming behavior -- the
   methodology refinement is that climbs followed by no-show flushes and then re-engagement at
   NOOBS-bracket competitions are themselves the farm cycle.

2. **Trial-Support alignment is now 18/18 over weeks 6+7**. Of 4 Support pre-actioned candidates
   this week, our trial confirmed BAN for all 4 at confidence 9-10/10. This is independent
   validation that the calibrated methodology now catches what Support catches -- and is in fact
   sometimes more conservative (the Lay_D14S downgrade from week-5 was correctly bounded by
   sample size; this is a positive False-Negative test).

The watchlist-to-ban escalation rule fired cleanly twice this cycle: `rabolio41100` (week-6
watchlist holdover) and `maminapokorny83` (week-5 watchlist holdover) both showed clear NOOBS
flavor change this week and went in without further deliberation.
