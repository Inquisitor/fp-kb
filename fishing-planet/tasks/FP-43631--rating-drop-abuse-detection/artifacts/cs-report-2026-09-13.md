# FP-43631 week-19 -- Community & Support handoff, sweep 2026-09-07..2026-09-13

12 accounts were reviewed and 7 were banned from competitions until 2026-10-12. The 3 TSVs are
the data for the shared sheet, one tab per platform as usual.

The cohort has shrunk from 17 last week to 12. Too early to read anything into that. The one time
this was watched closely -- a deliberate pause in enforcement around week-14 -- the numbers came back
to where they started within a fortnight, with an entirely different set of people and with the
amount being extracted more than doubling at the same headcount. That was a pause and this is not,
so the two are not comparable, but it is the reason a smaller list is not taken as progress.

Rating bands, since the tables use them throughout: **NOOBS** is rating 0-100, **MIDDLES** is
101-1000, **TOPS** is 1001 and above.

## Reading the tables

The columns are the same as last week and in the same order. Nothing was added or moved. If you kept
last week's tab, this one lines up with it.

**`Verdict`** has 2 values only. **BANNED** means banned from competitions, **WATCH** means reviewed
and not banned this time.

A reminder of the columns added last week, since this is the first full cycle using them:
**`Unproductive`** is absences and zero-score entries together, with **`UnproductiveSharePct`** their
share of everything the player signed up for; **`RatingFromUnproductive`** is the rating lost through
them. Read that last one against **`RatingFromProductivePlay`**, which is the net rating from
competitions the player started and scored in — and which replaced the older `RatingFromRealPlay`
under a new name because its meaning changed: it no longer counts zero-score entries.
**`ZeroScore`** itself is older than that and has been in the table since week-17.

Cautions on columns that are easy to misread:

- **`MaxRatingAtStart`** is the highest rating the player carried *into* a competition, not his peak
  for the week. A win lifts him immediately afterwards, so the real peak is usually higher.
- **`Rank`** is a profile field, not a leaderboard placing. Where this report mentions a position on
  the weekly board it comes from the leaderboard and will not match this column.
- **`RatingFromUnproductive`** is the rating the system charged, which near zero is more than the
  rating actually lost: a penalty larger than the remaining rating simply stops at zero. 3 of the
  players below have entries like that, so their shedding figures read a little worse than the real
  movement.
- **Blank is not zero** in the lifetime columns. AppL33 has no lifetime gold recorded and
  AdmiralAckbar98 no lifetime bronze; those cells are empty because the value is absent, not because
  it is nought.

### Rows that will look odd

- **FOGGIA1920** shows `IsBanned = true` with an end date of **2026-06-01**, in the past. That is an
  expired ban from an earlier action and is not in force -- the flag simply stays set after a ban
  lapses. He is WATCH this cycle.
- **Pilou62** carried the same expired flag before this sweep and now shows our own date of
  2026-10-12.
- **AppL33**'s bracket splits do not add up to his totals: `Played_NMT` accounts for 24 of his 26
  starts and `Prizes_NMT` for 3 of his 4 prizes. Those cells are built from the rating a player
  carried into each competition, and for a small number of entries that rating is not recorded at
  all, so the split silently drops them. Read both split columns as a floor rather than a total.

## Who was banned, and the short version of why

All 7 are first offences by our records, so all carry 4 weeks, to 2026-10-12.

- **KovlekPlayz** (Xbox) -- flagged last cycle and left on watch; the pattern then doubled. Prizes
  went from 5 to 9, unproductive entries from 8 to 21, registrations from 13 to 32. All 9 of his
  prizes were taken in the bottom bracket. He did rise above it once during the week, to 117 on a
  competition he played and won, and was back below within the day.
- **ZellyRolled** (Xbox) -- 3rd on the Xbox weekly board by wins, 6 wins, 7 of his 8 prizes taken in
  the bottom bracket. His rating rose above 100 6 times during the week and on 5 of those it was
  back underneath before his next contested competition. He entered 8 of his 9 competitions from
  below the line; the exception is the sixth rise, which he did not shed -- he played on at 117 and
  won, finishing the week at 164.
- **Pilou62** (Steam) -- competes and cashes inside MIDDLES, which on its own is not our target. What
  decided it is where he would be without the rating he threw away: he began the week at 975 and
  earned 399 by playing, which puts him above 1001 and therefore in the top bracket, while he
  actually finished at 848 and took all 5 of his prizes below that line. 32 of his 59 registrations
  produced nothing.
- **Suna4Y** (Steam) -- the steepest fall in the group: 60% of his registrations produced nothing and
  he finished the week at zero having entered it at 83. All 5 prizes taken in the bottom bracket, and
  5 of his 9 lifetime prizes were won inside this one week. 2 of his 3 drops below 100 came
  from playing badly rather than from absence, which is not something this task treats as an offence;
  the rest of the pattern is what carried it.
- **Mr.Twin** (Steam) -- competes across 2 brackets, takes all 4 of his prizes in the lower one.
- **AdmiralAckbar98** (PS) -- 62% of registrations produced nothing; competes half in each bracket
  and takes 3 of 4 prizes in the lower one.
- **sidelong-beak10** (PS) -- the heaviest zero-score case in this group, though not the heaviest
  we have handled: Myky0576 last week had 27 in a single week. This one entered 7 competitions in the
  charge week and finished them with nothing scored, against 16 absences, and across the preceding
  fortnight the figure is 17. Entirely inside the bottom bracket, never above 79.

## Not included: anti-cheat

Anti-cheat triggers appear in the same logs and are deliberately left out of this report. They form
no part of the decisions above, which are about competition rating only, and naming any individual
for them would need a comparison against ordinary players that we do not currently have. The counts
across this group range from 38 to 670 with no way to say which of those is unusual.
