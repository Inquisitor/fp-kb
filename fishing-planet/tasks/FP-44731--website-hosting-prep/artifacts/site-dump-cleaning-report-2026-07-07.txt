1. PURPOSE OF THIS REPORT
=================================================================
web-2026-07-07-clean.zip is a dump of the old static Fishing Planet
website, from which content pages (ToU, Privacy Policy, Game Rules,
etc.) need to be pulled for the new site being built by an external
studio. Secrets and files unrelated to the site have already been
removed from the dump (see section 6). Everything else is unchanged
from the original.

This file describes WHAT REMAINS in the archive: the structure, which
pages are current, which are duplicates/drafts, and what to watch out
for when migrating the text to the new site.

=================================================================
2. IMPORTANT: TWO DIFFERENT PRODUCTS IN ONE DUMP
=================================================================
The site serves two different products, and their legal documents
must NOT be mixed up:

- Fishing Planet - the main free version of the game.
  Folders without the "r" suffix: console/, pc/, mobile/, nintendo/, xbox/

- The Fisherman - Fishing Planet - a paid Retail version (sold on
  discs), with its own economy and exclusive waterbodies.
  Folders with the "r" suffix: consoler/, pcr/, xboxr/

Some pages share the SAME <title> but have DIFFERENT body text
(e.g. GameRules.htm and GameRulesPC.htm) - see section 5.

5. THINGS TO WATCH OUT FOR WHEN MIGRATING THE TEXT
=================================================================
- SAME <title>, DIFFERENT TEXT: GameRules.htm and GameRulesPC.htm both
  carry the title "Game Rules - FISHING PLANET", but the body text
  differs (different minimum age, different terminology, different
  abuse-report email). Do not deduplicate by title - compare by file
  path.

- <TITLE> BUG: consoler/eula/index.html and xboxr/eula/index.html have
  <TITLE>Game Rules...</TITLE> instead of an EULA title (looks like the
  Game Rules page was copied as a template and the title was never
  updated). The body text itself is genuine EULA text - only the
  browser-tab title is wrong. Take this into account when migrating
  (use the body text, not the <title> tag).

- STALE PRIVACY POLICY FOR THE RETAIL VERSION: consoler/privacy and
  xboxr/privacy are dated 2018-05-25, while the free version's Privacy
  Policy was updated in 2023 (2025 for Nintendo). If the Retail version
  is still being sold, this may be a compliance gap (outside the scope
  of this archive) - the text may need to be updated rather than simply
  copied over to the new site.

- TYPO IN EMAIL ADDRESS: all 14 tos/index.html files inside consoler/
  (root + all 13 locales) list support@fishingplane.com in the Forum
  Rules section (missing the letter "t" - a dead address).

- WRONG SUPPORT DOMAIN: pc/GameRules.htm and pcr/GameRules.htm ask
  players to report violations to support@fishingbeta.com (looks like
  a beta/staging domain, not the live support address).

6. WHAT WAS REMOVED FROM THE ORIGINAL DUMP (for context, in case the
   studio asks about missing files - their absence is intentional)
=================================================================
- ap/ (entire folder) - contained a tool for forging a signature for a
  third-party software license with a hardcoded private key, a test
  diagnostic ASPX page, and files completely unrelated to the site
  (LED-controller software, DVR/IP-camera firmware).
- u_ex210527.zip - actually a raw IIS log file (156 MB) containing
  visitor IP addresses and query strings from 2021-05-27 - personal data.
- FP_files/main.fc97045f.js.download and FP.html - production code and
  a page belonging to a third-party service, QRfy.com, unrelated to
  Fishing Planet.

Everything else from the original dump (428 files) has been kept
unchanged.
